# AGRINI (अग्रिणी) — Dance Club of South Asian University

> **Rooted in tradition. Restless in rhythm.**

Official website and interactive portal for **AGRINI**, the university dance club of **South Asian University (SAU)**.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features
- **Hero Doorway Transition**: Kinetic scroll-driven archway opening into the dance collective.
- **Vector Nataraja Heritage**: High-precision vector SVG silhouette framing classical dance heritage.
- **Memory Lane**: Horizontal sideways gallery featuring major performances including **UNESCO** and **ISA** (International Students' Association), with full-resolution click-to-expand lightbox modals.
- **Sacred Mandala Art**: Authentic 16-fold lotus chevron sacred mandala with rotating GSAP animations.
- **Responsive & Accessible**: Seamlessly adapts across mobile, tablet, and 4K displays with custom cursor interactions.
